E-Borrow

A Web-Based Barangay Alabang Equipment Lending System

The E-Borrow System is a web-based platform designed to modernize and optimize the equipment borrowing lifecycle for Barangay Alabang residents.

PROJECT TYPE Web-Based System
USERS Barangay Alabang Residents
STATUS Completed & Passed

PROJECT PURPOSE

To prevent on-site borrowing and reduce waiting time by allowing residents to check equipment availability and submit borrowing requests online.

SYSTEM FEATURES

  • Resident registration and login
  • Real-time equipment availability
  • Online borrowing requests
  • Borrow request management
  • Inventory monitoring

MY CONTRIBUTION

CAPSTONE PROJECT

SignSpeak-SafeCheck

A Filipino Sign Language Recognition System and Integrated RFID-Based Student Attendance & Safety Monitoring

SignSpeak-SafeCheck is a proposed system designed for the Philippine School for the Deaf. It combines Filipino Sign Language recognition with RFID-based student attendance and safety monitoring.

PROJECT TYPE Web & Mobile System
USERS Students of the Philippine School for the Deaf
STATUS Ongoing — Capstone Project

PROJECT PURPOSE

SignSpeak aims to help students improve their basic Filipino Sign Language knowledge while also helping school staff who may not have prior experience with sign language, helping reduce communication barriers.

SafeCheck aims to help parents and guardians monitor whether their child has arrived at or left the school premises through RFID-based Time In and Time Out monitoring and notifications.

SYSTEM FEATURES

  • Camera-based sign recognition
  • Filipino Sign Language recognition
  • RFID student cards
  • RFID scanner
  • Student Time In / Time Out
  • Parent/guardian notifications

MY CONTRIBUTION

My primary contribution to the project was documentation, including organizing and preparing the necessary project documents and supporting materials.
